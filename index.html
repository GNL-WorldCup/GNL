How it works

Format & rules

Tournament

  • 7v7 matches, no AI goalkeepers
  • 8 groups of 4 — win 3, draw 1, loss 0
  • Top 2 in each group reach the Round of 16
  • World Cup–style knockout bracket to the final
  • Squads need at least 10 players
  • Friendly matches before kick-off help staff balance the teams
